# Privacy policy (draft for a lawyer)

**DRAFT. Not legal advice. Not in effect.** This text is for the operator to send to a lawyer. It is not published, and the app does not link to it as a policy. Do not paste it into App Store Connect until a lawyer has read it and the operator has published the approved text at a real HTTPS URL.

Date of this draft: 26 October 2026.

---

**The Judge** is a card-grading aid. It runs on a server the operator controls. This draft says what that server stores. It is not a promise from a lawyer, and it is not in effect until the operator publishes it at a real URL.

**Who we are.** The operator of The Judge (the person who invited you to the beta). Contact: the email address published next to this policy.

**Scan images.** When you capture or upload a card, the app sends that image to the server so it can find the card and measure it. The server stores the image, a straightened copy of the card, and the measurements it computed. Those files exist so you can look at the grade again. They are not sold. They are not sent to a grading company. They are not used to train a model unless you turn on “Help improve The Judge,” and even then only the correction described below is eligible. The scan image itself is not copied into that correction file.

**Where scans live.** A scan stays on the home server until you have an account. It is not copied to a hosted service until that account exists and this policy is published. Until then, the server is only for use on the home network.

**The card you type.** The app does not name the card for you in this beta. You may type a name or player, a year, a set, a number, a game or sport, and a note. Those words are stored with the scan so you can find the card again. They are not a grade, and they are not a price. The card stays Unidentified until you type a name.

**Measurements.** Lengths are stored in millimetres. If the program measured a border, that number is an engine measurement. If you dragged a line, that number is yours. It is shown as “your estimate.” It does not replace the engine’s number, and it does not change the predicted grade. The predicted grade is the engine’s measurement only. It is not a PSA, BGS, SGC, CGC, or TAG grade.

**Assisted-correction examples.** If you drag a border line, the server stores an example: the scan id, the side, any candidate lines the engine had, your line in millimetres, the engine version and commit, and a consent flag. The flag is off unless you have turned on “Help improve The Judge” before you save. Examples with consent off stay on the device or on that server. They are not copied to a hosted bucket, a scan archive, or anywhere else. Turning the setting on does not change examples you already saved. You can ask for those examples to be deleted even if consent was on.

**Accounts.** When accounts are on, you sign in with Apple. The scan is tied to that Apple account, so a deletion request can find your rows. Your inventory is separate from another shop’s inventory. A username with no Apple sign-in is not an account.

**What we do not do.** We do not sell personal information. We do not put advertising trackers in the app. We do not send your scans to a grading company. We do not treat your estimate as the engine’s measurement. We do not store a price for the card.

**How long we keep it.** Scans and examples stay until you delete the card in the app or you ask us to delete the account. Deleting the account removes that account’s images, grades, and correction examples. Backups, if any, are deleted on the same request as the live copy. Another shop’s scans, and the operator’s own home-bench scans that are not on your account, are not part of your deletion.

**Deletion requests.** Email the contact address above from the same address as your account, or from the address you used for TestFlight, and say which scans or examples to delete. Ask for “everything” if you want the account, the images, the grades, and the correction examples removed. We will delete them from the live server and from any copy we made. We will write back when that is done. A request does not delete a grade that a grading company issued. We do not hold those.

**Children.** The beta is not for anyone under 16.

**Changes.** If this text changes, the date at the top changes, and the app will point at the new URL after it is published.
